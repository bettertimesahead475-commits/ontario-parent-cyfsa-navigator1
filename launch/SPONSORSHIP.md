# Sponsorship and funding opportunities (research list, no contact made)

## Independence policy (adopt before accepting anything)
- Sponsors do not influence legal content, analyzer output or the lawyer directory.
- No sponsor may be a party that commonly appears against parents in these cases (for example, a children's aid society) without a published conflict review.
- Sponsor logos stay out of reports, PDFs and the analyzer.
- No parent data is shared with sponsors, and every sponsorship is disclosed on a public page.

## Categories to research (check eligibility first; many require non-profit or charitable status)
| Category | Examples to research | Usual fit | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access-to-justice grants | Law Foundation of Ontario programmes; federal Justice Canada funding streams | Plain-language legal information | Often for non-profits; a partnership with a clinic may be required |
| Community foundations | Ontario Trillium Foundation; local community foundations | Family support, digital inclusion | Usually charities or non-profits only |
| Legal-tech and innovation programmes | University legal-innovation hubs and law school clinics | Pilots, evaluation, student help | In kind rather than cash |
| Professional associations | Family law sections of law associations | CPD speaking slots, newsletters | Speaking slot rather than sponsorship |
| Corporate social responsibility (non-legal) | Banks, telecoms, insurers with family or digital-access programmes | Free-access seats for low-income parents | Check for conflicts |
| In-kind tech credits | Cloudflare, Supabase, Google for Startups programmes | Hosting and inference cost relief | Applying is free; check terms before enrolling (no paid commitment) |

## Offer to sponsors (draft)
- **Free-access fund:** sponsor-funded Case Access seats, given out by partner clinics, never by us choosing individual parents.
- **Content partnership:** a sponsor funds an article series. Content is reviewed by independent counsel, with the sponsor credited at the foot of the page.
- **Research partnership:** an anonymised, aggregate usability study run with a university ethics board. No case content.

## Sponsor pack outline
1. Problem: parents in child protection matters often meet the process without counsel. Cite public sources only, such as Legal Aid Ontario or court statistics, with links.
2. What CYFSA Navigator is and isn't: information and preparation, not advice.
3. Privacy and safety controls (redaction, source tagging, synthetic-data testing).
4. Programme options and costs: per-seat cost from the pricing API ($149 CAD per month for Case Access).
5. Governance: the independence policy above.
6. Reporting: counts of seats issued and used, with no personal data.

Owner action: confirm the company's legal form. Charitable or non-profit eligibility decides most of this list.

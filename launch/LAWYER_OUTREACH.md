# Lawyer and legal-community outreach (draft, not sent)

## Position
The product helps parents arrive at a lawyer's office organized: a timeline, source-tagged findings and a Lawyer Case Brief PDF.
It does not give advice and does not replace counsel. For lawyers, the pitch is **better-prepared clients and less intake time**, not referrals for sale.

## Compliance limits (confirm with Ontario counsel before any outreach)
- **Referral fees:** the Law Society of Ontario's conduct rules restrict paying referral fees to non-licensees.
  So: **no per-referral or per-client fees from lawyers.** A flat, disclosed listing or advertising fee, or a free listing, is the safer model (decision D3).
- **Lawyer advertising rules** apply to how listings describe lawyers. Show the lawyer's own approved wording; no "best" or "specialist" claims unless the lawyer is certified.
- **CASL (Canada's anti-spam law):** commercial email needs consent. Implied consent from a publicly listed business address applies only when
  the message is relevant to the recipient's role and the address was published without a "no unsolicited email" statement.
  Every message needs sender identification and a working unsubscribe link. Keep consent records.
- **No case data** goes to a lawyer except what the parent exports and sends themselves.

## Target segments, in priority order
1. Family and child protection lawyers who accept Legal Aid Ontario certificates.
2. Community legal clinics and student legal clinics (they do intake for unrepresented parents).
3. Duty counsel offices, as information only. Ask for nothing; offer the free guides.
4. Family law sections of local law associations (CPD or lunch-and-learn talk).
5. Indigenous child and family well-being organizations. Partnership only on their terms, with content review.

## Pilot offer (draft)
- 10 lawyers or clinics, 90 days, at no charge.
- Each gets a demo with a synthetic file, the Lawyer Case Brief format, and a feedback form.
- In return, we ask for written feedback on whether the brief saves intake time. Any quote is used only with written permission and in their exact words.

## Objections and answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "AI gives parents wrong ideas" | Every finding quotes the parent's own document with a page tag, and the brief is framed as questions for counsel |
| "Confidentiality" | The parent controls exports; redacted copies are available; no documents are shared with us for marketing |
| "Is this unauthorized practice?" | Information and organization only; every output carries the not-legal-advice notice. Get an Ontario lawyer's opinion before launch (owner action). |

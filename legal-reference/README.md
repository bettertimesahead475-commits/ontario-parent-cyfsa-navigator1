# Legal Reference: CYFSA Full Text

`CYFSA_full_text_2026-06-24_consolidation.txt` is the complete, verbatim, official text
of the Child, Youth and Family Services Act, 2017 (S.O. 2017, c. 14, Sched. 1), extracted
from the official Government of Ontario document at
https://www.ontario.ca/laws/docs/S17014_e.doc (via the e-Laws statute page's raw file link,
which — unlike the interactive e-Laws page — does not require JavaScript and is not blocked
by robots.txt like CanLII).

Consolidation period: July 1, 2026 to the e-Laws currency date. Last amendment: 2025, c. 12,
Sched. 1. This is the CURRENT, in-force version as of 2026-08-29, not the original 2017
introduced bill (Bill 89) — that historical version is a different document and should not be
used as a citation source.

## Verified section numbers (confirmed directly against this file, 2026-08-29)

These were previously either wrong in the app or marked "unverified" pending statute access:

- **Part IV** (ss. 68-73.3) = First Nations, Inuit and Métis Child and Family Services.
  NOT "Part X" (Part X is Personal Information/privacy — unrelated).
- **s. 88** = "Time in place of safety limited" — the actual 5-day rule (previously
  miscited in the app as s. 94(5); s. 94 is about adjournments, unrelated).
- **s. 78** = "Legal representation of child" — right to counsel (previously miscited as
  "Section 41," which is actually about accountability agreements).
- **s. 100** = "Society's plan for child" — the real Plan of Care requirement (previously
  miscited as "s. 51," which is actually about transferring charitable property during
  society restructuring).
- **s. 101(5)** and **s. 109(2)(d)** = the actual First Nations/Inuit/Métis extended-family
  and same-community placement preference language (previously a vague, unverified
  s.70/109/116 cluster).
- Already-correct citations, reconfirmed: s. 74(2) protection grounds, s. 81 apprehension
  without warrant, s. 104 access orders, s. 121 appeal rights.
